# LazyFit – A Lightweight Fitness App for the "Too Lazy to Move"

LazyFit is a mobile fitness app designed for people who struggle with motivation, time management, or feel overwhelmed by traditional workout routines. Built with Flutter and Firebase, LazyFit provides a fun, easy, and sustainable way to build healthy habits—5 minutes at a time.

## Features

- Targeted for "lazy exercisers": Designed for young adults, students, and office workers who lack time or motivation.
- Smart workout guidance: Daily exercises ranging from 5–15 minutes with step-by-step animations.
- Gamified PK (Player Kill) mode: Compare your fitness stats with others across four dimensions — Endurance, Burst, Strength, and Flexibility.
- Training calendar: Review daily history with color-coded feedback and monthly statistics.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Flutter (Dart)
- **Backend**: Firebase Firestore & Authentication
- **State Management**: setState, GetStorage
